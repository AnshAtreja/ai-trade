# Phase 10 Roadmap — Kronos / Financial Time-Series Intelligence

**Status:** PLANNED

## Goal

Evaluate Kronos as an experimental forecasting component against simple deterministic baselines and out-of-sample data.

## Planned scope

OHLCV input adapters, zero-shot benchmarks, direction/return/volatility horizons, confidence calibration, regime/period comparisons, and ablation evidence.

## Guardrails

Kronos is optional research infrastructure. It cannot place orders or silently change Phase 8 strategies.
