# Phase 06 Roadmap — Strategy Engine

**Status:** COMPLETE

## Purpose

Convert scanner candidates and market context into deterministic, versioned, explainable setups.

## Delivered scope

- Strategy interface and registry.
- `breakout:v1`, `breakdown:v1`, `momentum:v1`, and `opening-momentum:v1`.
- Bounded tick history and 1m/5m aggregation.
- Entry, stop, target, reward-risk, invalidation, conditions, reasons, and setup identity.
- Strategy API, scheduler, signal evaluation, persistence, and web page.

## Explicit non-goals

No orders, sizing, AI, Kronos, news, or unified decision engine.
