# Phase 12 Roadmap — Event Intelligence, News & RAG

**Status:** PLANNED

## Goal

Ingest and retrieve company, market, macro, exchange, results, regulatory, and corporate-action events with provenance and risk classification.

## Planned scope

Source ingestion, deduplication, timestamps, retrieval, event taxonomy, evidence links, and unknown-risk handling.

## Guardrails

No unaudited headline may directly authorize a trade. LLM assistance must remain bounded by retrieved evidence.
